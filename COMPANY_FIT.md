# DeepMap — Company and Team Fit

**Snapshot:** 2026-11-13

## Product thesis

A terminal tool that groups latitude/longitude coordinates into coarse map cells and stores simple terrain profiles.

These are potential employer and skills matches, not claims that any company uses, endorses, funds or has requested this repository.

## Google DeepMind

**Relevant public technical areas:** Geospatial/scientific computing and research engineering.

**Potential role families:** Software Engineer, Data Engineer, Research Engineer, Applied Scientist or ML Engineer, depending on the team's current openings.

**Evidence to build here:** Python CLI and JSON persistence. Decimal latitude/longitude values are mapped into one-degree grid identifiers; terrain metadata is associated with cells and a rule-based friction score is calculated. This is a coarse exploration prototype, not a navigation or safety system.
